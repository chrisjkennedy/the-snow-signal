Recommendations are ranked by each resort's outlook score — its own snowfall, cold-day reliability and year-to-year consistency from ERA5 reanalysis, plus elevation, adjusted for the climate signal currently driving its region. Costs are planning estimates in USD per person, not quotes: airfare in particular varies more than the rest of the budget combined. Lift prices come from published 2025-26 rates where available and regional bands otherwise; every resort links to its own site, which is the only authority on price and opening dates. Après ratings are reputation-based, drawn from published resort and nightlife guides rather than measured, so treat them as a steer.
