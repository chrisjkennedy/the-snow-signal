Key Wildcards — Apply to Every Region, in Every Phase

What can override the seasonal signal

ENSO Intensity
Weak vs. moderate vs. strong is a dramatically different scenario, in either direction — a strong El Niño or strong La Niña amplifies every regional signal on this page. The live ONI reading above is the single best number to track through the season.
Sudden Stratospheric Warming
SSW events disrupt the polar vortex and send cold air south for 4–6 week windows, capable of overriding the ENSO pattern in either phase. Watch for these mid-winter regardless of the seasonal thesis.
Snow Level vs. Snowfall
A wetter pattern also often means a warmer airmass. A storm delivering 3 feet of water at a warm snow level produces rain at 7,000 ft and powder above 9,000 ft. Elevation of the freezing level matters as much as total precipitation.
Background Warming Trend
Climate change compresses the effective snow window at lower elevations in every ENSO phase. High-altitude resorts are increasingly the only reliable option — a structural shift that operates independently of any single season's ENSO cycle.

This page is a living tool, not a one-time forecast: the live-status line, signal bar, and every region's bull/bear call are all keyed off data/oni.json's current ENSO phase, so the page's guidance flips automatically as the climate signal moves between El Niño, La Niña, and neutral — the name and framing stay put either way. Live data: NOAA CPC Oceanic Niño Index (refreshed via scripts/update_oni.py, not real-time in-browser due to CORS); NRCS AWDB (SNOTEL / manual snow courses) for % of median snowpack; Open-Meteo for 7-day snowfall forecasts. Static analysis: region-level ENSO-phase correlations are drawn from NOAA CPC's historical ENSO-snowfall composites, IRI/CCSR ENSO probability research, JMA historical snowfall data (1954–2014), and Severe Weather Europe ENSO analysis. Pass affiliations (Epic/Ikon/Indy) reflect known season rosters and change year to year — badges marked unverified should be checked at the official pass site before purchase. Après ratings are reputation-based, drawn from published resort and nightlife guides rather than measured, so treat them as a steer. Links marked ↗ are partner/affiliate links.
